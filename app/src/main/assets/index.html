Android notes

Face ID and Apple Pay are Apple-only, so on Android they become fingerprint or Google Pay. Android already draws its own mic and camera dots. Live activities (rides, flights, scores, delivery) would come from other apps' notifications, so the real app can show some but not all of them. Everything here is a simulation to tune the look and feel.
